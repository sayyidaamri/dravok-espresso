Understanding machine specifications
Boiler
Water held at temperature. Larger boilers recover faster between drinks and take longer to warm up.
Voltage
Match this to your outlet. 220V machines need a dedicated circuit in most homes.
Depth
Front to back, including the drip tray. Leave two more inches for the hose and cord.
Clearance
Space between the spout and the tray. Check it against your tallest cup.

Fit can vary by outlet, cabinet height and plumbing. Confirm your complete counter before installation.
